Research Statement

I am interested in efficient and secure computer architectures, particularly specialized AI accelerators, data movement and memory systems, hardware security, and RISC-V microarchitecture. My work focuses on understanding architectural bottlenecks through RTL design, verification, synthesis, implementation, and quantitative evaluation.
