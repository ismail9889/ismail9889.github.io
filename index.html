I am interested in efficient and secure computer architectures, particularly specialized AI accelerators, data movement and memory systems, hardware security, and RISC-V microarchitecture.

My work focuses on understanding architectural bottlenecks through RTL design, functional verification, synthesis, implementation, timing analysis, and quantitative evaluation.

I am particularly interested in understanding how microarchitectural decisions, memory organization, scheduling, data reuse, and security mechanisms influence the performance and efficiency of modern computing systems.
